A grass set from the same source was shipped briefly and has been removed. The
plateau's surface is generated in src/art/groundTexture.ts instead: a
photographic grass map gives the whole island the relief of gravel, and reads
as wet rock the moment a highlight crosses it.

Modifications from the originals
--------------------------------

Each set ships as three files rather than the five the source provides.

  *-color.webp    The Color map, re-encoded to WebP.
  *-normal.webp   The NormalGL map, re-encoded at higher quality than the
                  others, because banding in a normal map reads as visible
                  faceting across a lit surface.
  *-orm.webp      AmbientOcclusion packed into red and Roughness into green,
                  with blue unused.

The packing is the glTF convention, and it is what lets one file fill two
material slots: three.js reads ambient occlusion from the red channel and
roughness from the green channel, so the same texture is assigned to both
aoMap and roughnessMap. Metalness would occupy blue, but nothing in this
world is metal, so it is left black.

The Displacement maps are discarded. Nothing here is tessellated or
parallax-mapped, so they would be weight with no effect.

All maps are resized to 1024x1024. Colour and ORM are encoded at quality 86,
normals at 92. Total is roughly 3.9 MB.

Stone additionally has its albedo levelled at load time, so the palette drives
its colour and the photograph only supplies grain. That happens in
src/art/textures.ts, not here, and the reasoning is in that file.
